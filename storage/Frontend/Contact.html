Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions. Still need help? Use the contact form above.

You can track your order by clicking on "Track Order" in the top utility bar, or by visiting your Order History under My Account. Enter your order ID (e.g., CC-2024-78432) or the tracking number sent to your email/SMS. You'll see real-time updates including pickup, in-transit, and delivery status.

We offer a 7-day easy return policy on most products. To initiate a return, go to My Orders → Select the item → Click "Return". Our delivery partner will pick up the item from your doorstep at no extra cost. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to your original payment method or ClickCodex Wallet.

Delivery times vary by location and product: Same-day delivery in 15+ metro cities (order before 12 PM), 2-day delivery in 200+ cities, and standard delivery (3-7 business days) across all other pincodes. Orders above ₹999 qualify for free standard delivery.

Don't worry — if the amount was deducted but the order didn't go through, the amount will be automatically refunded to your source (bank/UPI/wallet) within 5-7 business days. If you don't receive the refund after 7 days, contact us with your transaction ID and we'll resolve it within 24 hours.

We welcome sellers of all sizes! Register at sellers.clickcodex.in with your GSTIN, PAN, and bank details. Our onboarding team will verify your documents within 48 hours. Once approved, you can list products, set prices, and start selling to our 50 lakh+ customer base. We charge a competitive commission with no hidden fees.
