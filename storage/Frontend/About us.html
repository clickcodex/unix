Our Story

ClickCodex was born in 2020 from a simple observation: millions of Indians in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities were paying premium prices for products that should be affordable. Our founder, Arjun Mehta, experienced this firsthand while trying to buy genuine electronics at fair prices in Ratlam.

What started as a small operation from a single room — curating genuine products from verified sellers — quickly grew into something much bigger. Customers didn't just want products; they wanted trust, transparency, and speed. We delivered on all three.

Within two years, ClickCodex expanded to serve over 500 cities across India, partnered with more than 10,000 sellers, and built a customer base of over 50 lakh happy shoppers. But we're just getting started.

Today, ClickCodex isn't just a marketplace — it's a movement to democratize e-commerce in India, ensuring every citizen, regardless of their location, has access to the same quality, selection, and prices as someone in a metro city.

Our Journey

From a single room to a nationwide platform — every milestone was driven by our customers' love.

January 2020

The Spark

ClickCodex was founded in a small room in Ratlam, MP. With just 3 team members and 100 products, we set out to change how India shops.

March 2021

100 Cities Milestone

Expanded delivery to 100 cities. Launched our seller onboarding program and crossed 1 lakh registered customers. Series A funding secured.

August 2022

The Big Leap

Launched ClickCodex App with 10 lakh downloads in the first month. Introduced same-day delivery in 15 metro cities. Team grew to 500+ members.

January 2023

50 Lakh Customers

Crossed 50 lakh happy customers. Partnered with 10,000+ sellers. Launched ClickCodex Pay — our own UPI payment solution for faster checkouts.

2024 & Beyond

The Future is Now

AI-powered product recommendations, drone delivery pilots in select areas, and expansion to 1000+ cities. We're building the future of Indian e-commerce.
