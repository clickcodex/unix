Return Policy

How to return products you're not satisfied with

schedule Updated: 15 Jan 2026

info 7-Day Return Window: At ClickCodex, you can return most products within 7 days of delivery. We want you to be completely satisfied with every purchase.

Eligible Products for Return

  • All electronics and mobile accessories in original, unopened packaging with all seals, tags, and accessories intact.
  • Fashion apparel and footwear that are unworn, unwashed, and have all original tags attached. Undergarments and swimwear are non-returnable for hygiene reasons.
  • Home and kitchen products in original condition with all packaging materials, manuals, and warranty cards.
  • Beauty and personal care products only if the seal is unbroken and the product is unused.
  • Books, if received in damaged condition or with missing pages.

Non-Returnable Items

  • Downloadable software, digital gift cards, and prepaid recharge vouchers.
  • Customized or personalized products (e.g., engraved items, custom-printed apparel).
  • Perishable goods including groceries, flowers, and food items.
  • Products with tampered or broken seals on beauty, health, and personal care items.
  • Innerwear, socks, and swimwear due to hygiene considerations.

How to Initiate a Return

  1. Go to My Orders in your ClickCodex account and select the order you wish to return.
  2. Click on "Return" and select the reason for return from the dropdown menu.
  3. Choose your preferred return method: Self-Ship or Schedule a Pickup (available in select pin codes).
  4. Pack the product securely in its original packaging. Affix the return shipping label if self-shipping.
  5. Once the returned product is received and inspected at our warehouse, you will be notified within 48 hours.

lightbulb Tip: Keep the original packaging until you're sure you want to keep the product. This makes returns faster and hassle-free. Take photos or a video while unpacking — this helps in case of damage claims.

Cancellation Policy

When and how you can cancel your orders

schedule Updated: 08 Jan 2026

Cancellation Timeframes

  • Before Dispatch: Orders can be cancelled free of charge at any time before the product is dispatched from our warehouse. Go to My Orders → select the order → tap Cancel.
  • After Dispatch but Before Delivery: Cancellation may be possible but is not guaranteed. Contact our support team immediately. If the shipment is intercepted in transit, a cancellation fee of ₹50 may apply to cover reverse logistics.
  • After Delivery: Orders that have been delivered cannot be cancelled. Instead, please use the Return process as outlined in our Return Policy.

Non-Cancellable Orders

  • Personalized or custom-made products once production has begun.
  • Digital products including gift cards, software licenses, and subscriptions.
  • Pre-orders that have entered the shipping preparation phase.
  • Orders placed during flash sales or limited-time deals where cancellation was explicitly disabled at the time of purchase.

Partial Cancellation

For orders with multiple items, you can cancel individual items while keeping others. The refund for the cancelled item(s) will be processed independently, and delivery of the remaining items will continue as scheduled.

schedule Auto-Cancellation: If an order remains unpaid for more than 30 minutes (for certain payment methods like net banking) or if payment fails after multiple attempts, the order will be auto-cancelled and you'll be notified via SMS and email.

  • <>Payment Processors: Payment data is processed through PCI-DSS certified partners (Razorpay, PayU, etc.). We do not store full card details.

  • Sensitive data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 encryption.

Refund Conditions

  • Products must be returned in their original condition — unused, unwashed, with all tags, accessories, and packaging intact.
  • Refunds for damaged or defective products are processed as full refunds including any shipping charges paid.
  • For buyer's remorse returns (no defect), a reverse shipping fee of ₹50–100 may be deducted from the refund amount, depending on the product category and size.
  • If a replacement is not available, a full refund will be processed automatically.
  • Partial refunds may be issued if only part of the order is returned or if the returned product shows signs of use or damage not reported at the time of delivery.

ClickCodex Wallet Refunds

Refunds credited to your ClickCodex Wallet are instant and can be used for any future purchase on the platform. Wallet balance has no expiry date. You can also transfer wallet balance to your bank account (NEFT) from the Wallet section — this transfer takes 3–5 business days.
